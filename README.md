# LedgerX Showroom

Car showroom module for LedgerX: one or many showrooms, new and used cars.

- **Stock and branches:** cars per showroom, transfers between showrooms, status flow Draft → In Preparation → Ready → Reserved → Sold → Delivered.
- **Purchase and preparation:** acquisition posted to inventory, extra costs capitalised, inspection checklist.
- **Booking and sale:** bookings with a deposit that holds the car; Car Sale creates the invoice, cost of sale and margin.
- **Financing, three ways:** the showroom's own installments, transfer to a bank or finance company, or a Lending loan.
- **CRM and commissions:** link leads to cars, test drives, monthly salesman commission into payroll.
- **Reports:** Showroom Stock, Car Sales Margin, Installments Due; dashboard cards and charts.

See `docs/INSTALL.md`. Requires erpnext and hrms.
